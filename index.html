About Me

3D Artist
focused on craft

I'm a 3D Artist based in Buenos Aires, Argentina, specialized in character creation, prop modeling, cinematic work, and rigging. I'm passionate about pushing the quality bar of every project I work on.

Whether it's sculpting a high-poly character, texturing props, or building a clean rig, I aim to bring both technical precision and artistic vision to every deliverable.
