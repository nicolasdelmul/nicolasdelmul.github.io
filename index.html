About Me

3D Artist
focused on craft

I'm a 3D Artist based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. I graduated from the Universidad de Buenos Aires (UBA) in 2022 with a degree in Image and Sound Design — a career oriented toward cinema, narrative, and audiovisual storytelling.

I later completed a Master's in Character Modeling for Film and Video Games at Animum Creativity School, where I deepened my skills in high-quality character creation for production pipelines. I'm currently about to begin a Hard-Surface for AAA Games course to further expand my technical toolkit.

Specialized in character creation, prop modeling, cinematic work, and rigging, I aim to bring both technical precision and artistic vision to every deliverable.
